## Bio

Journalist focused on drug policy and criminal justice.

- - -

## Teaching

Over the past decade, I have taught courses at the Columbia University Graduate School of Journalism, the Craig Newmark Graduate School of Journalism at CUNY, The New School, and The School of The New York Times.

These courses include reporting, writing, ethics, and local news, and advising master’s theses. 

For the 2025-26 academic year at Columbia, I served as managing editor of the school’s pop-up Substack publication [CollegeWatch](https://journalism.columbia.edu/news/collegewatch), which covered higher education under the Trump administration.

Also at Columbia, I spent five years researching the implications of technology platforms for news publishers as a fellow at the Tow Center for Digital Journalism, and, until 2021, led its flagship project [Platforms and Publishers](https://towcenter.org/#/type/research/category/platforms-publishers). You can find my published research [here,](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center_reports/platforms-and-publishers-end-of-an-era.php) [here](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center_reports/the-platform-press-at-the-heart-of-journalism.php), and [here](https://www.cjr.org/author/nushin-rashidian).

## Reporting

I am the co-founder of [Cannabis Wire](http://cannabiswire.com/), a news organization that covers the global cannabis industry through the lens of policy, research, and regulation.

Our publishing partners have included [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2016/may/29/cannabis-native-american-marijuana-sales-farming-washington), [Quartz](https://qz.com/africa/1744366/lesotho-is-perfect-growing-cannabis-but-not-regulation), and [Documented](https://documentedny.com/2019/01/16/in-ices-arsenal-cannabis-convictions/), and we’ve hosted events in New York City with state and local lawmakers.

*Cannabis Wire* has been featured in (among other publications) [The Sun](https://www.thesunmagazine.org/articles/27207-high-time) and [Digiday](https://digiday.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2020/03/digiday_issue17_03010_digital.pdf), and it has been written about or cited in publications like [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/membership/archive/2019/01/25-years-reefer-madness/580351/), [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/medical-marijuana-users-are-being-shut-out-of-public-housing/), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/chrisroberts/2021/02/09/tobacco-giant-altria-is-pushing-marijuana-reform-on-congress-and-state-lawmakers/), and [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/cuomo-legal-marijuana-likely-wont-be-included-new-york-budget-2020-3).

As one of the first journalists on the cannabis beat and an authority on the subject, I have also provided my expertise over the years to other journalists on how best to cover cannabis, including writing a [Nieman Reports](https://niemanreports.org/why-cannabis-coverage-needs-to-be-a-serious-beat/) feature on exemplary cannabis journalism and creating a Poynter Institute course called Covering Cannabis.
